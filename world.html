03 / Beyond Vanta Quay

Beyond the waterfront.

Leave Vanta Quay behind and explore painted districts, ruins, and dungeons with your crew and Mote.

Start in Vanta Quay, a waterfront district of neon signs and painted storefronts. Beyond the city, the route leads through hostile approaches, side paths, and places like Asterion. Look for treasure and recovery points between encounters.
